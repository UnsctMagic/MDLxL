# Hands and feet

At unit distance a mitten-shaped fist and a separate thumb are often enough. Start with a tapered 4-sided wrist, a wider palm block and a wedge or bent pair of faces for the thumb. Use about 8–24 triangles for an ordinary hand. Paint finger separations and knuckles; model separate fingers only when the requested pose or silhouette needs them. A fist gripping a shaft should wrap around it with no visible daylight, while keeping room for the wrist to bend.

Start a boot with a narrow ankle ring, wider heel and broad toe wedge, around 8–20 triangles. A thick sole is a silhouette plane or one shallow layer, not a stack of bevelled rings. Paint toe caps, stitches, straps and sole highlights. Keep feet thick enough to read from the game camera and wide enough for the body stance.

Human boots use squared toes, orcs broad rough feet or wraps, elves narrow wedges, undead exposed ankle/bone contrast. Vary toe length, heel width, thumb angle and glove cuff rather than adding fingers to every hand. On an existing model keep bindings and inspect planted frames; a clean static mesh is not proof that the hand follows the weapon or the boot stays on the ground.
