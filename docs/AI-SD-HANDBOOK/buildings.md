# Buildings

Start with the footprint, wall mass, roof and one race-defining vertical feature. Each simple roof slope can be two triangles; use additional corners for a real gable, eave or change of pitch. Named native roof territories are small, but do not treat a single roof bone's count as the entire building. Paint tiles, masonry blocks, boards, windows and small trim. Put geometry on eaves, roof peaks, large posts, door recesses and silhouette changes.

Human buildings use masonry and steep coloured roofs, orcs sturdy timber/hide and spikes, elves curved wood/living growth, undead heavy stone and sharp dark silhouettes. Use checked native wall, roof, wood and stone regions with consistent texel scale. A roof strip must follow the painted tile direction; do not stretch one tile across the entire roof.

Vary roof pitch, footprint, tower position and entrance mass rather than scattering ornament. Preserve existing birth/death/upgrade/working states and foundation geometry on edits. Check the game-angle silhouette, underside/ground join and selection footprint. Do not add particle glows or working machinery unless requested.
