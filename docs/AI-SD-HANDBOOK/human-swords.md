# Human longswords and short blades

Build a fresh tapered blade from a broad base, one optional change of taper and a tip. Give it a shallow diamond or wedge section so both sides have volume. Around 8–24 triangles often establishes the blade, up to 32 for a deliberately shaped tip or fuller silhouette. Use a 4–5 sided grip, a blocky crossguard around 8–20 triangles and a 4–5 sided pommel around 4–16. A basic held sword can often stay around 40–90 triangles. It does not need 400 triangles just because it is shown alone.

A useful starting proportion is blade about 65–75% of length, grip 15–22%, guard width 25–40% of the whole length; thicken edge and guard until readable beside a unit. Paint the fuller, cutting edge, grip wraps and small studs. The native Footman steel/guard sheet is a candidate only after region preview. Map the two blade sides to the same steel island where appropriate; keep long edges aligned to its painted highlight.

Vary taper, point shape, crossguard angle, grip length and pommel outline. Human service weapons are plain steel and leather, a holy blade may add restrained gold and one clear symbol. Never add a gem, floating emblem or rune band by default. Avoid huge round handles, rows of tiny bevels, an ultra-thin blade and a texture stretched across several unrelated islands.

## Measured evidence and limits

blade: 91 named-bone territories; triangles median 39, P10–P90 14–556; 15 detected planar-ring samples (median 6 sides). shaft: 56 named-bone territories; triangles median 10, P10–P90 2–70; 3 detected planar-ring samples (median 6 sides).

Unit models only, excluding portrait files: blade: 39 territories, median 20 triangles; shaft: 33 territories, median 22 triangles. Heroes and differently named bone territories are still mixed; inspect the comparable role.

These counts come from [installed game measurements](../AI-SD-PART-DATA.json). A bone territory may be one segment or several joined parts; portraits, scenery and variants differ. Targets above are construction guidance, not measured percentiles or universal caps. Unknown classifications stay unknown. No source geometry is supplied.
