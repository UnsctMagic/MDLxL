# Undead runeblades

Start with the same economical solid blade construction as a human sword, then change the outline: hooked tip, broken taper or one broad jagged edge. Around 12–40 triangles for the blade is a useful starting target; the grip stays 4–5 sided. Put a bone-like guard or thick iron crosspiece at the handle join, not a row of repeated skull spheres.

Paint runes, rust, hairline cracks and the bright cutting edge. Use native Death Knight blade/bone regions if a checked region fits; green glow is an accent and belongs only when requested or inherent to the subject. A runeblade can remain readable with a few painted marks and no particles.

Vary spine curvature, notch placement, guard silhouette and the balance of dark metal to bone. Keep motifs coherent with the named faction. Do not make every evil weapon the same black sword with an eye, add full-size sculpted teeth over a texture-sized band, or import a famous existing blade. Inspect backside UVs, guard thickness and attachment clearance.

These counts come from [installed game measurements](../AI-SD-PART-DATA.json). A bone territory may be one segment or several joined parts; portraits, scenery and variants differ.
