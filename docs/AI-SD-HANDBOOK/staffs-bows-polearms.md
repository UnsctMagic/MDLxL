# Staffs, bows and polearms

A staff is a 4–5 sided shaft with two or three deliberate bends and one clear head shape. Paint grain, wrapping and tiny bindings. A spear is a straight shaft and a wedge blade; a halberd adds a single thick axe outline and a short rear hook. Use about 8–24 triangles for a simple shaft, increasing only for a visible bend or articulation. Finials should read through outline, not many tiny rings.

A bow uses a few tapered segments following its flexed silhouette; model enough depth to survive rotation. The string can be a very thin solid strip or deliberately supported material setup, not an accidentally invisible edge. Make the grip and arrow clearance readable. Preserve the source's animated bow mechanics on edits.

Human staffs can use wood/steel, orcs rough wood/bone, elves curved wood and restrained silver, undead a bent dark shaft and a bone motif. Vary head silhouette, shaft bend, bow limb taper and grip location. A wizard's hat is handled in cloth/hair and armour; do not add a staff merely because the prompt asks for a hat.
