# Effects

Judge effects by coverage, timing, silhouette and readability beside a unit. Many useful native effects need very few mesh triangles because billboards, particles and ribbons do the work. Do not apply a humanoid body budget or make every spell into a dense glowing object.

Use the existing emitter, ribbon and material APIs. Native effect sheets are often alpha cutouts or additive sprites; a measured alpha channel alone does not determine filter mode. Check the intended blending and animation cells by preview. Paint broad luminous masses and purposeful falloff, not photographic noise.

Vary emission silhouette, scale, timing and one colour accent according to the request. Preserve named events, attachment points, global sequences, texture animations and visibility when editing. Check start, peak and fade against a unit silhouette and from different angles. A still image cannot verify timing, and the editor renderer cannot certify Warcraft gameplay.
