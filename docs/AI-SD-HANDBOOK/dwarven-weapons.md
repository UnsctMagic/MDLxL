# Dwarven hammers and maces

Use a rectangular hammer head with a slightly tapered striking face and perhaps one clipped rear corner. A head around 12–32 triangles and a 4–5 sided grip around 8–24 gives useful volume. For a mace use a low-sided core and a few broad fins; model the fin silhouette and paint small studs or engraving.

The hammer head should feel heavy: head length about 25–40% of overall weapon length and a thick shaft set into it. Steel, bronze/gold trim and leather read well. Look for checked Mountain King or Rifleman regions rather than photographic metal. A painted highlight across the top and dark contact at the socket establish weight without multiple bevel rings.

Vary square versus tapered head, offset rear spike, grip length and trim placement. Pick one dominant feature. Avoid a smooth round mallet, a ring around every grip segment, embossed text meshes and intersecting handle ends visible through the top. Check its held point and both striking faces.
