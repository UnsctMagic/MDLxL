# Skulls and bones

Use a low-sided cranium, a flattened wedge for the face, two deliberate eye-socket shapes and a thick lower jaw. Around 40–80 triangles is a useful basic skull target informed by the head study; the measurements do not isolate every native skull. Do not combine a high-resolution sphere with many little tooth boxes. Paint teeth divisions, tiny cracks and dark recesses. Model the jaw silhouette, cheek recesses and the largest nasal/sockets only as needed.

Use a checked native Skeleton bone region for general bone; a complete skull face needs the correct skull island or an original painted diffuse. Do not fit every face to one bone-colour rectangle and expect eyes to line up. Use warm lit bone with cool/dark cavities. A horn or tusk can start with 3–5 sides and two or three tapering rings, around 6–24 triangles.

Make each new skull fresh: choose different cranium height/width, jaw width/projection, cheek angle, socket spacing and broken-tooth silhouette. Change several of these, not just colour or scale. Keep sockets recessed and the jaw connected, inspect the underside/back and avoid detached eyeballs or a row of paper teeth.

For repeated skull requests, vary at least three independent features: long versus squat cranium, wide versus narrow jaw, cheek projection, socket spacing and the placement of one missing or broken edge. Keep the skull identity and broad planes intact. Adding the same teeth or horns to an unchanged sphere is not a distinct construction.

## Measured evidence and limits

head: 890 named-bone territories; triangles median 66, P10–P90 5–158; 26 detected planar-ring samples (median 4 sides). horn: 59 named-bone territories; triangles median 20, P10–P90 2–30; 4 detected planar-ring samples (median 4 sides).

Unit models only, excluding portrait files: head: 373 territories, median 40 triangles; horn: 11 territories, median 2 triangles. Heroes and differently named bone territories are still mixed; inspect the comparable role.

These counts come from [installed game measurements](../AI-SD-PART-DATA.json). A bone territory may be one segment or several joined parts; portraits, scenery and variants differ. Targets above are construction guidance, not measured percentiles or universal caps. Unknown classifications stay unknown. No source geometry is supplied.
