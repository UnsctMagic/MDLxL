# MDLxL 0.35.0 - Pivots in sight

**Movement and bones**
- **Double-click a part of the model** in the preview (Movement, Bones or Animations): the bones that part hangs on get selected and **Focused Skeleton** turns on, even when Bones and Focused Skeleton were off.
- **Pivot cross**: every bone Focused Skeleton highlights shows its own X (red), Y (green) and Z (blue) axes through its pivot. The cross turns with the bone, so rotations are easy to follow during playback.
- **Controller type** now sits in the Controller heading as a small dropdown. **Multiselect from List** is gone because multiselect is always on: Shift-click adds, Ctrl-click removes, a plain click selects just that object, in the preview and the Object list alike.

**Vertices**
- **Zoom Align** (Shift+1, pink): with the Zoom tool, box one selected vertex, then zoom in. The rest of the selection slides until every point lines up behind the pink vertex from your viewpoint, keeping its distance from the eye, so a crooked surface becomes flat as seen from that view. Anchor (1, green) and Align are one at a time.

**Editor**
- **Save as > Model name**: set the name stored inside the model, separate from the file name, right in the Save as window.
- **Glow Up** shows EMTR's **Pause / Play** button under the preview (Space does the same), so both glow ends can be placed while the animation holds still.
- **Welcome window**: the first-launch Auto update question is now a small MDLxL welcome window with a short fanfare instead of a Windows warning box.
- **Help > About** is a real About: version, what MDLxL is, who made it, thanks and the components it is built on.
- **Help > Check model** is removed; it only showed an empty list.
- **Translations caught up**: Spanish, Russian and Chinese now cover the texts added over the last releases. Chinese gained about 3,000 texts that were still in English.

**Update** Open Settings > Configuration > Search for updates > Update and restart, or download the full portable ZIP from the MDLxL page.
