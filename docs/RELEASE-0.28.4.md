# MDLxL 0.28.4 - Hotfix

**Fixes**

- Bones mode has one compact Bones / Helpers / Refs checkbox row below the Object dropdown and above Multiselect. Your visibility choices are remembered when reopening the editor.
- Refs can be selected, moved and edited again. Create > Ref adds a reference point and makes it visible immediately. In VIS, Bones, Helpers and Refs stay enabled and the checkbox row is hidden.
- Bones stay green, Helpers purple and Refs pink. Each Appearance preset owns its colors. Text on white uses deep green, purple and pink with a fine black outline for readability; text colors are adjustable under Appearance > Lists & folders, and 3D marker colors under Bones & skeleton. Dropdowns keep their normal appearance, with a small black Object-picker arrow.

**Update**  Open Settings > Configuration > Search for updates > Update and restart, or download the full portable ZIP from the MDLxL page.
