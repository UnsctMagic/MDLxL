# MDLxL 0.25.1 - Hotfix: Shift plane lock

## Vertices

- With Workplane off, holding Shift while dragging now locks the move to the chosen XY, ZX or YZ plane, the same as MDLVis. Before, an angled camera could push the selection out of that plane.
- Held vertices no longer jump while you drag with Shift, or when you press or release Shift mid-drag. They carry on from where they are.
- The same fix applies to bones dragged in the vertex view, to Paint, and to free node and POSE drags in Movement.
- Quad View keeps its own Shift lock: hold Shift and move sideways or up to lock that direction.

## Update

In 0.25.0, open Settings > Configuration > Search for updates > Update and restart.
