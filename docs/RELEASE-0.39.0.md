# MDLxL 0.39.0

- **Settings:** new layout with small animated previews. UV Wrapper and 3D Rotate/Zoom have full settings with presets (MDLxL, MDLVis, Freeform, Manipulator) and Each piece.
- Every hotkey and mouse drag key can be rebound, and preset files share them with your drag settings and appearance.
- **Toolbar:** switch between Graphic, Trim and Hidden, with a command launcher on Ctrl+F2. Hide extras also hides hero glow and part-time effects.
- **Citadel Paint:** **New base coat** has a new layout with far fewer seams and more pixels for heads. **Detail** sets how many pixels a selected part gets. Also new: pen pressure, Smoothing, Symmetry and layer opacity.
- **Calculate extents:** one calculator in the model inspector, OptimizeXL and Save As. Portrait backgrounds, glows and additive meshes no longer raise the health bar.
- **EMTR and Glow Up:** Rotate handles on X, Y and Z. **Add ribbon** makes a native weapon trail shown only during the swing, and ribbon fields update it live.
- **Nodes manager:** emitter visibility keys. **FX:** several checked geosets are fitted as one surface, emitters preview before **Add**, and ten new looks.
- **OptimizeXL:** **Remove exact redundancy** removes repeated end keys, constant tracks and collapsed triangles. Alternate stances compare only with their own stance.
- **Bones:** **Wiggle** (J) swings selected bones to reveal bad binding.
- **Modeling:** Ctrl + Resize moves pieces at full size. Right-click **Uncouple** and turn off **Nuke** to separate without moving.
- **UV Wrapper:** pick any model material, and big pieces no longer outrun the mouse.
- **POSE and ANIM:** elbow and knee Rotate matches other handles. Dragging empty space turns the ANIM camera.
- **Auto Corpse:** wings, tails, 254+ nodes and looped effects.
- **AI console:** separate edits combine as one Undo, and the review no longer freezes the window.
- **Saving:** verification problems are warnings and never block a save.
- Frame intervals show total frames. Highlight Geoset works both ways in Animations.

Update through Settings > Configuration > Search for updates > Update and restart, or download the full portable ZIP from the MDLxL page.
