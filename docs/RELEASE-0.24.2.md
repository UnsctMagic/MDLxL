# MDLxL 0.24.2 - Restrict on by default, faster attachments, safer MDL saves

## POSE

- Restrict is now on by default: moving a hand or foot keeps the body still. Click Restrict to switch it off and let the limb lead the body.

## Movement

- Showing Attachment no longer slows XL view down. Models with many attachments play about as smoothly as with Attachment off.

## Saving

- Saving as MDL now keeps a few rare stored values that were lost before: unusual material filter modes, extra geoset animation flags and white ribbon emitter colors. Reopening the MDL gives back the same model.

## Update

In 0.24.1, open Settings > Configuration > Search for updates > Update and restart.
