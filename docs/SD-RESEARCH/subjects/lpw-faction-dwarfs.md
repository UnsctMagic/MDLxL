# Dwarfs: artillery construction rather than humanoid proportions

Scope: 5 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/dwarfs-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Dwarf Cannon and all four Dwarf Organ Cannon versions use broad metal-covered wheels, low compact carriages, large muzzle openings, blue vertical bands and gold rings or face ornaments. The single cannon has one dominant barrel; the organ cannon has five aligned dark muzzles.

Sources: Dwarf Cannon (`lpw-gluma-145021`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (`lpw-gluma-145231`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (Full Barrage) (`lpw-gluma-145232`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (Single Shots) (`lpw-gluma-145233`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (Small Barrages) (`lpw-gluma-145234`).

All four organ-cannon variants have exactly 878 triangles, 14 geosets and three catalogue geoset texture references. Their filenames and sequence variants describe different firing patterns, and their MDX sizes range from 184,277 to 322,173 bytes. This is a direct example of file size varying greatly without a triangle-count change.

Sources: Dwarf Organ Cannon (`lpw-gluma-145231`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (Full Barrage) (`lpw-gluma-145232`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (Single Shots) (`lpw-gluma-145233`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (Small Barrages) (`lpw-gluma-145234`).

The Dwarf Cannon is 582 triangles. In the pictures, the organ version changes not only the barrel count but also the central vertical rib mass. The repeated five-muzzle silhouette makes the extra 296 triangles visually intelligible.

Sources: Dwarf Cannon (`lpw-gluma-145021`), Dwarf Organ Cannon (`lpw-gluma-145231`).

## What this sample does not establish

All five Dwarf entries are artillery, and four are versions of one organ-cannon design. This set cannot support claims about dwarf body proportions, beards or infantry armour.
