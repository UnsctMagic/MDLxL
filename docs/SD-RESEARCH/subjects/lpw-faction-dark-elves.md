# Dark Elves: pointed silhouettes and deliberately exposed pale skin

Scope: 10 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/dark-elves-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Witch Elf, Beastmaster and Slavemaster combine pale faces and arms with black or near-black clothing, purple vertical cloth and sharp triangular shoulder or head outlines. The Witch Elf uses white swept hair, broad curved swords and gold chest/shoulder trim; the Beastmaster uses dark studded shoulders and a coiled whip. The Slavemaster adds a gold horned crown and a taller pointed shoulder collar.

Sources: Witch Elf Unit (`lpw-gluma-159912`), Dark Elf Beastmaster unit (`lpw-gluma-160458`), Dark Elf Slavemaster unit (`lpw-gluma-160460`).

The hero/unit pairs change the glow and death presentation without needing a much larger visible body. Beastmaster Hero totals 580 triangles versus 602 for the unit; Saurus-style death-set comparison is explicit here too: the unit has Decay Bone and Decay Flesh, the hero has Dissipate. The hero’s sampled visible geometry is 578 versus the unit’s 562.

Sources: Dark Elf Beastmaster unit (`lpw-gluma-160458`), Dark Elf Beastmaster hero (`lpw-gluma-160459`).

Generic Ballista and Dark Elf Ballista keep the same basic crossbow-on-wheels layout. The Dark Elf version introduces upright blade-like prongs, purple limbs and heavier riveted wheel covers. Total geometry rises from 720 to 756 triangles. The equivalent bolt-thrower pair rises from 816 to 852, the same 36-triangle difference.

Sources: Generic Ballista (`lpw-gluma-160790`), Dark Elf Ballista (`lpw-gluma-160792`), Generic Bolt Thrower (`lpw-gluma-160791`), Dark Elf Bolt Thrower (`lpw-gluma-160793`).
