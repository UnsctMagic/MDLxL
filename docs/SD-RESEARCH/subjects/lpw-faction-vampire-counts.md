# Vampire Counts: a faction built from deliberately different body types

Scope: 6 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/vampire-counts-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Graveguard carries the undead identity with a concealed head, thin exposed bone, long polearm and ornate tall shield. Von Carstein Vampire and Von Carstein Dread Knight expose a pale face above upright plate and a long sword. Strigoi Vampire instead uses an enlarged muscular bare torso, long arms, claws and a dark animal-like face. Necrarch Vampire uses a hanging black robe and narrow exposed hands. These examples contradict a single universal vampire body proportion.

Sources: Graveguard (`lpw-graveguard`), Von Carstein Vampire (`lpw-von-carstein`), Von Carstein Dread Knight (`lpw-von-carstein-dread-knight`), Strigoi Vampire (`lpw-strigoi-vampire`), Necrarch Vampire (`lpw-necrarch-vampire`).

Von Carstein Vampire and Dread Knight both contain exactly 1,535 triangles, yet the catalogue has 30 versus 35 geosets and their files differ by 4,850 bytes. Their published images distinguish the cloth and armour treatment rather than a larger silhouette. Equal triangle totals therefore do not imply equal material partition or file contents.

Sources: Von Carstein Vampire (`lpw-von-carstein`), Von Carstein Dread Knight (`lpw-von-carstein-dread-knight`).

Graveguard uses the Human campaign Footman, Human campaign tree, Undead inventory cover, Undead buildings and multiple creature sheets in one model. These are measured references in the actual MDX, not a list of suggested substitutes. Its credits name the shield as projected and the gargoyle head and helmet wings as Blizzard sources.

Sources: Graveguard (`lpw-graveguard`).

Blood Dragon concentrates red on the torso, shoulders and weapon while the limbs and hanging cloth stay dark. Its credited shoulder pads/breastplate come from a dragon-scale armour source, distinct from the skull belt, vampire face and helmet sources. In this collection the Blood Dragon silhouette is an armoured warrior, whereas the Strigoi silhouette is the body itself.

Sources: Blood Dragon (`lpw-blood-dragon`), Strigoi Vampire (`lpw-strigoi-vampire`).
