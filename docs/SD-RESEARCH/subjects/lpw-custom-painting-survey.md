# Painting comparisons across every supplied Gluma/HerrDave custom image

The 81 model-specific custom-texture references for models attributed to Gluma or HerrDave resolve to 42 distinct images. Every one is viewed below; most are 256 by 256 pixels, with Ratling and Stormvermin using 512 by 256. Model attribution is preserved from the catalogue; it is not independent proof of who painted every source stroke. The extra Diablocar override supplied for Maximal models is recorded separately and is not counted as a Gluma/HerrDave custom painting.

## Where the numbers attach

The companion `lpw-region-pixel-measurements.json` contains the real UV footprint, source alpha histogram, area fraction and material layers per component/texture pair. These records distinguish overlapping UV reuse from unique allocated pixels: adding all part fractions can exceed 100% because multiple parts deliberately share paint. Source alpha does not by itself equal team-colour share; filter mode, layer order and visibility determine the composited result. The four-model detailed painting chapter and `custom-painting-parts.json` provide direct part labels for the Saurus, Black Orc and Witch Hunter examples.
