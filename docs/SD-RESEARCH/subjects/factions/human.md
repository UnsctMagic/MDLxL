# Human infantry, dwarf specialists and masonry

![Reviewed SD sources](D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/warcraft-factions/human.webp)

Footman and Knight give the clearest plate-armour comparison. Footman separates a large white-rimmed shield, red team-colour shoulder caps and a pale helmet face from a darker torso. Knight repeats bright edged armour and gold decoration across both rider and horse. The Knight's 45-triangle horse covering carries painted medallions, and its five-triangle straps carry painted studs. A rich mounted silhouette therefore does not imply a separate mesh for every gold detail.

Footman's nine-triangle shield front includes the white rim as paint; its eight-triangle central boss supplies actual relief. The helmet shell/visor costs 47 triangles and the face/moustache another 44. Two nine-triangle shoulder caps obtain their rounded reading partly from curved painted highlights. Those particular counts explain how Footman spends more on recognition around the head than on a rim around the shield; they are not universal armour budgets.

Mountain King and Rifleman make dwarf identity differently from Footman's enclosed head. Mountain King's 123-triangle helmet/face/beard assembly is supplemented by a twenty-triangle moustache and separate four-triangle eyebrows; Rifleman's face/beard costs 94 with a separate 23-triangle moustache. Their large facial silhouettes remain distinct from the surrounding armour or hood. Mountain King's twelve-triangle box hammer has painted inner bevels, whereas Rifleman's weapon begins with a 32-triangle stock/barrel body plus separate fittings and alpha cards.

TownHall, HumanBarracks and Farm keep building materials in broad readable zones: pale masonry, wood framing/doors and a large roof. TownHall and HumanBarracks use red team-colour roof fields in these captures; Farm uses fixed yellow thatch and a red door. HumanBarracks can carry a large roof panel in four triangles and a tower roof cap in four, while Farm's chimney pots use thirteen triangles including a five-sided opening. Painted brick joints and thatch strands supply detail without corresponding masonry blocks or individual straws.

This reviewed human set is not one body proportion: Rifleman is dominated by hood, beard and a long gun; Mountain King by beard and paired blunt weapons; Knight by the combined horse/rider. Their common bright metal and bordered colour fields are visible in the named sheets below, but those material choices do not erase the different silhouettes. The High Elf/Blood Elf sources are compared separately because they share a gameplay roster without sharing the dwarf head proportions.
