# High Elves, Blood Elves and civilian faces

![Reviewed SD sources](D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/warcraft-factions/elf-commoner.webp)

HighElfArcher, Priest, Sorceress, HeroBloodElf and BloodElfSpellThief share pale skin, pointed ears and strong bright metal or gold edging in the reviewed sample. HighElfArcher's light hair and pale face contrast with the purple skin and darker hair of the reviewed Night Elf Archer, while the cloak-and-bow arrangement remains visually comparable. Both sampled bodies have 417 triangles; this number alone is not proof of identical underlying geometry.

Priest uses pale robe surfaces with gold edging around a narrow upper body and long staff. Sorceress combines pale skin, flowing hair, team-colour cloth fields and bright shoulder/arm borders. HeroBloodElf broadens the silhouette with large gold-scrollwork shoulder shapes and a dark flared robe. BloodElfSpellThief combines a tall red-gold shield, long pale blade and a narrow armoured body. Their sampled body counts are 399, 427, 569 and 432, each with one visible diffuse path. The dark robe and ornate gold edges of HeroBloodElf are not the same material arrangement as Priest's broad white surfaces.

VillagerMan, VillagerWoman and VillagerKid show a separate civilian use of facial proportion and clothing. The adult man wears a pale broad-shouldered shirt over brown lower clothing; the woman has a pale blouse and a long brown skirt; the child has a disproportionately prominent pale head, short torso and simple brown clothing. Their sampled bodies are 311, 325 and 216 triangles. Those are observed meshes, not a proposed recipe for scaling an adult to make a child.

The official unit tables classify these civilian models as commoner while the elf models sit in the human gameplay roster. The visual comparison keeps both facts: roster membership provides searchable source context, while the particular skin, hair, cloth and silhouette in the named source models provides the art evidence. Detailed ear cross-sections and individual costume-region labels are available only where separately reviewed; this profile does not extrapolate them across every elf or civilian model.
