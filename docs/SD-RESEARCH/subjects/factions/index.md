# Warcraft SD profiles and official roster membership

The ten picture-led profiles compare 86 directly viewed native SD models. Component construction claims refer to the separately reviewed annotations; whole-model visual review does not imply that every component has been named. The machine-readable record contains the full installed SD official race memberships in addition to the selected visual samples.

The installed race field is not a clean art label. Bandit has human, undead and creeps assignments; HeroPitLord appears under undead and creeps; ShimmeringPortal is assigned human in a unit row. Those exact mappings remain searchable instead of being corrected into an invented taxonomy. The profiles choose and name the visual references explicitly.

The studies concern SD art, including any SD assets stored in newer MDX formats. They do not infer an art tier from the numeric file-format version. The comparison pages retain source texture paths because skin, gold trim, bone, cloth and foliage occupy different regions even on one sheet. Exact UV masks and part-linked material evidence live in the associated model/texture datasets and capture pages.
