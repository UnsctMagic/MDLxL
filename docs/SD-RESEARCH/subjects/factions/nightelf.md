# Night Elf: pointed humanoids, animals, pelts and living architecture

![Reviewed SD sources](D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/warcraft-factions/nightelf.webp)

Archer, EX_Dryad and Huntress share pale purple skin and sharply pointed ears in the reviewed captures, but differ in carrier anatomy. Archer stands on two legs with a broad team-colour cloak and long angular bow. EX_Dryad joins an elf upper body to a deer-like four-legged body. Huntress places the rider above a dark cat. This variety means an elf profile must retain what the body sits on; the overall unit height alone cannot describe the proportions.

Archer's body/head/arms/armour form one 307-triangle component, with a 24-triangle recurved bow, twelve-triangle quiver and 33-triangle cloak. EX_Dryad's head is 114 triangles and its animal body 148; ears cost six each and hooves nine each. Its spear's leaf-shaped end uses two perpendicular four-triangle cards. Leaf imagery is therefore sometimes painted cutout geometry, rather than a thin solid leaf or a fully rounded spearhead.

Huntress's reviewed rider body is 232 triangles and the mount body 221. Four two-triangle alpha cards carry the cat's claws, and an inspected fang has two oppositely wound faces sharing three positions. These details make the mount's face and feet readable without small closed solids everywhere. Huntress's dark cat, silver rimmed glaive and purple rider also differ strongly from EX_Dryad's warm brown animal body and green leaf details.

DruidoftheClaw uses a 112-triangle hood with a visible bear mouth around the head, while the alternative full bear body is a separate 366-triangle component hidden in the sampled caster pose. HeroDemonHunter's head/hair is 141 triangles plus a 27-triangle topknot; its body is 119. Long ears, long hair and curved paired weapons create its elf silhouette without requiring foliage on the body.

TreeofLife and AncientofWar carry faces, bark masses, root-like limbs, green canopy patches and heavy stone-like plates. Their 698 and 736 sampled body triangles belong to articulated living architecture, rather than a small hut with tree paint. The reviewed Night Elf set thus uses at least four distinct material arrangements: Archer's cloak/skin/metal, EX_Dryad's skin/fur/leaves, DruidoftheClaw's animal pelt and the Ancients' bark/foliage/stone. The sheets and mapped regions in the table preserve those differences.
