# Naga: tail carriers, fins, bright metal and marine architecture

![Reviewed SD sources](D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/warcraft-factions/naga.webp)

NagaMyrmidon and NagaRoyalGuard show a broad finned head/shoulder mass over a coiled tail, with blue-green skin and a large bright gold weapon. Their sampled bodies are 563 and 581 triangles, each using one visible diffuse sheet. NagaSiren narrows the upper body and carries a long tail with an extended fin edge; HeroNagaSeawitch adds a larger ornamented head/shoulder arrangement and a bow. The latter two are 604 and 822 triangles. These are whole-body observations; they do not establish an isolated fin or tail budget.

The reviewed NagaMyrmidon, NagaRoyalGuard and NagaSiren captures place cyan/green painted skin next to warm yellow-gold edges. Sharp head fins, weapon points and tail fins project beyond the main torso. HeroNagaSeawitch preserves the long tail carrier while adding broad dark-gold ornament and a more prominent ranged weapon. Red identity areas in this red-player preview must be distinguished from the underlying fixed blue-green skin.

SnapDragon belongs to the official naga roster but has a low four-legged, long-bodied profile instead of a humanoid torso and tail. Its blue body has a pronounced dark dorsal fin line and long pointed snout. TempleofTides uses an upright serpentine form within a stone platform, CoralBed combines a pillar and branching coral colours around a low base, and TidalGuardian places a turquoise creature in a raised basin with tall curved corner forms. Those named examples connect marine motifs across creatures and buildings without assuming every naga object is a snake-bodied person.

The reviewed units each use one visible diffuse path, while TempleofTides uses three and CoralBed and TidalGuardian two. The building and unit counts include their sampled visible geometry only, excluding hidden construction/death alternatives. Detailed fin cross-sections and painted-versus-solid ornament boundaries were not manually traced for this Naga sample; the profile records visible silhouettes, actual sheet assignments and numeric model evidence, leaving those narrower component claims unresolved.
