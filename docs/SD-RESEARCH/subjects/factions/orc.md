# Orc roster: exposed skin, tusks, pelts and large wood forms

![Reviewed SD sources](D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/warcraft-factions/orc.webp)

Grunt and HeroBladeMaster both expose large green shoulder/arm masses, but the weapon and back silhouette distinguish them. Grunt pairs a compact heavy axe with blocky boots and a low head. HeroBladeMaster adds a long narrow blade, a tall bent banner pole and a broad emblem field. The banner costs twelve triangles; its gold emblem is paint. The 14-triangle pole provides its bend geometrically, so the outline survives even when the emblem is too small to read.

Grunt's connected head/jaw/tusk assembly is 130 triangles, with separate five-triangle ears and a two-triangle long facial tusk elsewhere. Its axe blade and haft are eight triangles each; the haft has three sides at the measured sections. Each eight-triangle shoulder plate carries the irregular pale fur border on the same painted surface. This is a concrete instance where facial mass and silhouette receive geometry while a furry edge on armour remains paint.

Tauren, Headhunter and WitchDoctor prevent an orc profile from collapsing into green skin. Tauren's bull head, torso and arms form a 234-triangle assembly; the fourteen-triangle totem carries painted end grain, wraps and large glyphs. Headhunter and WitchDoctor use blue skin, long tusks and pointed ears in the reviewed captures. Headhunter's head is 113 triangles and its tusks fifteen each; WitchDoctor's head is eighty and tusks nine each. Their shared motif is elongated facial anatomy, but the measured parts differ substantially.

Shaman's pale animal pelt produces another distinct outer contour. The body/pelt assembly has 262 triangles, with nine-triangle claw ornaments and four-triangle collar strips carrying painted chain links. GreatHall and WatchTower repeat large timber forms at architectural scale: GreatHall's radial roof planes and pale ribs surround a central peak; WatchTower exposes a tall braced timber support under a six-triangle, seven-position roof. The tower's roof is a geometric six-sided pyramid, while wood grain and binding marks are painted.

HeroBladeMaster's two-triangle hoop earring, Tauren's two-triangle nose-ring card and Shaman's painted chain strips show three ways to suggest small curved metal forms without fully round geometry. The chain, ring and banner observations belong to these specific sources. LowPolyWorks' Aspiring Champion uses the same broader idea of selective detail but spends 36 triangles on individual raised studs; that owner example should not be substituted for the native orc counts.
