# Small paint selections, large changes in reading

All examples in this chapter have a directly inspected fitted component, a second angle, carrier context, and the exact UV triangles. Percentages count native-resolution pixel centres inside the union of the real UV triangles; they are not rectangle recommendations or fractions of the mesh. The source panels show RGB paint, including RGB stored beneath transparent pixels. Authored alpha, team-colour layers and tint are retained in the dataset and in the fitted result.

## Two very different chains from interface metal

Knight of Khorne with flail uses seven separate closed 48-triangle chain links: 336 triangles in this chain alone. Each link's measured topology has no boundary edges; its central slice has two closed loops, consistent with a thick ring rather than a painted card. The paint comes from the expansion glue-screen metal-pieces sheet. Nurgle Champion uses six separate four-triangle chain surfaces arranged as three links: 24 triangles, from the non-expansion metal-pieces sheet. The second fitted view makes the flat surfaces visible. These are two deliberate representations of a small chain, not a 4–48-triangle universal chain rule. Their different thickness, number of links and animation roles must remain attached to the numbers.

## A repeating light pattern is reusable at several geometric scales

Aspiring Champion carries six separate 36-triangle axe studs using InfernalCannon. The same model has four-triangle torso strap pieces using OgreLord, where the rivet dots are painted into the strip. Warrior of Khorne's two hammers use separate six-triangle gold studs from UndeadAirBarge: 16 on the left hammer and 48 on the right. These observations distinguish a painted dot, a small projecting stud and a denser rounded stud. ## Paint colour is only one of the colour controls

Knight of Nurgle's SpaceMarine skull discs multiply their source RGB by (0.568627, 1, 0.254902), and also have a team-colour layer. Their green appearance therefore does not mean the source image contains green skulls. Knight of Khorne's UndeadShipyard crossguard uses neutral white geoset tint; its red is supplied by the team-colour layer through the paint's alpha. Blood Dragon's chainmail uses a strongly red multiplier on HumanCampaignFootman. These three mechanisms must be recorded separately when searching for a material: selected paint, authored per-geoset colour, and team-colour contribution.

The source panel and result panel deliberately show that difference. The authored-colours dataset records the sampled RGB multiplier, sampled alpha, animation presence and team-colour/glow layer presence for all 130 LowPolyWorks models. Colour observations were rechecked after correcting animated MDX colour-channel interpretation; the 83 owner geosets with unequal animated red/blue values were all explicitly inspected in the corrected render.

## These observations do not prove historical provenance

A UV mask proves where a model gets its paint. A credit states an attribution. A geometric match is a different result. The dense SkeletonOrc-painted 218-triangle skull on Kurgan Warlord and the GargoyleSpire-painted Graveguard shield head must not be called unmodified native geometry solely because their sheets are native. Source matching, deformation and re-rig evidence are recorded separately. Small uncertain mask/helmet infill boundaries remain marked probable; connected armour-and-arm pieces stay compound labels.
