# Beastmen: horns and hooves unify several levels of armour

Scope: 20 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/beastmen-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Ungor Slave, Bowman and Spearman show the least armoured bodies: bare torsos, small horns, thin limbs and cloven feet. Gor Axe and Gor Flail enlarge the horns and add a round shield and shoulder metal. Bestigor adds taller spiked upper-body armour and banner-like vertical elements.

Sources: Bestigor Unit (`lpw-gluma-127546`), Gor Axe (unit) (`lpw-gluma-123554`), Gor Flail (unit) (`lpw-gluma-123552`), Ungor Slave (`lpw-gluma-133854`), Ungor Bowman (`lpw-gluma-99740`), Ungor with spear (`lpw-gluma-90822`).

Gor Axe unit has 805 triangles and Gor Flail unit 851. Their body and round shield remain close in the pictures; the flexible chain weapon creates a new outline for an increase of 46 triangles. This is a small, concrete weapon-family variation rather than a broad blade budget.

Sources: Gor Axe (unit) (`lpw-gluma-123554`), Gor Flail (unit) (`lpw-gluma-123552`).

Bray Shaman uses an animal skull-like head, long robe and a staff with two forked horn shapes. Bray Shaman of Tzeentch instead combines blue skin, purple hair, gold bands and a hooked gold staff. The Tzeentch unit is 716 triangles versus the ordinary Shaman unit’s 1,094; the mark remains readable without greater overall complexity.

Sources: Bray Shaman Unit (`lpw-gluma-137407`).

Maximal’s Minotaur, Minotaur Drummer, Centigor Spearman and Centigor Axethrower add distinct body plans to the collection. The two centigors have four hoofed lower legs and an upright human-like torso, whereas the minotaurs remain bipedal with a greatly enlarged chest and horn span. The two centigor totals are 2,377 and 2,609 triangles.

Sources: Minotaur (`lpw-maximal-144834`), Minotaur Drummer (`lpw-maximal-144833`), Centigor Spearman (`lpw-maximal-141710`), Centigor Axethrower (`lpw-maximal-141711`).

## What this sample does not establish

The 20 entries contain many hero/unit pairs. Differences between Gluma and Maximal are not automatically faction rules; they are named authors’ interpretations within the same collection.
