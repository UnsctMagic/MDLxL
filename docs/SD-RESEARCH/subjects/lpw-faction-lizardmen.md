# Lizardmen: distinct reptile body families under a shared ornament vocabulary

Scope: 26 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/lizardmen-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Saurus Warrior and Oldblood use deep blue scale fields, cream horns and claws, gold fittings and a bright turquoise shield. Oldblood expands the head with a gold crest and large cheek shapes; the body remains recognizably the same heavy reptile family.

Sources: Saurus Warrior (`lpw-gluma-137328`), Saurus Oldblood Unit (`lpw-gluma-140896`).

Skink Worker, Spear Skink and Blowpipe Skink have much thinner lower legs, large round eyes, a narrow long tail and tall coloured head fins. The Worker is not a scaled-down Saurus: its eye and head-to-body relationship are visibly different.

Sources: Skink Worker (`lpw-gluma-134840`), Spear Skink (`lpw-gluma-134398`), Blowpipe Skink (`lpw-gluma-134399`).

Kroxigor and Ancient Kroxigor broaden the jaw, hands and upper torso. Both carry a heavy two-handed weapon across the body; the Ancient adds a tall geometric gold-and-turquoise crest. Their unit totals are 925 and 1,154 triangles.

Sources: Kroxigor Unit (`lpw-gluma-146392`), Kroxigor Ancient Unit (`lpw-gluma-146394`).

Cold One Rider puts a blue rider and turquoise shield over an olive-green mount, giving two bodies separate colour blocks. The Feral Cold One alone is 480 triangles; the rider unit is 1,492. This is a real mounted-versus-unmounted comparison within one authored pack.

Sources: Cold One Rider Unit (`lpw-gluma-143396`), Feral Cold One (`lpw-gluma-143398`).

Blue and Orange Blowpipe Skink each have 753 triangles and an identical multiset of component shape fingerprints; Skink Skirmisher and Orange Skink Skirmisher each have 863 with the same result. Their published pictures differ strongly in skin colour. Shape reuse and palette variation are independently measurable here.

Sources: Blowpipe Skink (`lpw-gluma-134399`), Orange Blowpipe Skink (`lpw-gluma-134404`), Skink Skirmisher (`lpw-gluma-134400`), Orange Skink Skirmisher (`lpw-gluma-134402`).

Skink High Priest Hero and its Swimless version retain 954 triangles and matching component geometry. Removing six named Swim sequences reduces the file from 370,952 to 286,572 bytes. Skink Worker likewise keeps 776 triangles but loses 118,708 bytes with eleven Swim sequences absent. Animation scope is a major part of file weight.

Sources: Skink High Priest Hero (`lpw-gluma-135399`), Skink High Priest Hero (Swimless) (`lpw-gluma-135400`), Skink Worker (`lpw-gluma-134840`), Skink Worker Swimless (`lpw-gluma-134841`).

## What this sample does not establish
