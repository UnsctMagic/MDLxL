# Skaven: hunched bodies, long tails and weapon-sized technology

Scope: 6 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/skaven-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Clanrat, Stormvermin, Warplock Jezzail and Ratling Gunner retain projecting snouts, bent-legged posture, thin segmented tails and small exposed feet. Green cloth ties the examples together while the body and hands remain a dirty brown or pale flesh colour.

Sources: Clanrat (`lpw-gluma-80783`), Stormvermin (`lpw-gluma-82593`), Warplock Jezzail (`lpw-gluma-80903`), Ratling Gunner (`lpw-gluma-82702`).

The Ratling Gunner’s back apparatus and multi-hole muzzle dominate the silhouette; its 1,890-triangle total is over twice the Clanrat’s 720. The Jezzail is between them at 1,085 triangles and emphasizes an exceptionally long firearm instead of a bulky pack. The examples show three different allocations of geometric effort to readable equipment.

Sources: Ratling Gunner (`lpw-gluma-82702`), Clanrat (`lpw-gluma-80783`), Warplock Jezzail (`lpw-gluma-80903`).

Stormvermin raises the role with a broad upward-curving polearm blade, horned helmet and larger metal shoulder shape. Its 819 triangles remain much closer to Clanrat than to Ratling Gunner. The rank difference is concentrated in conspicuous extremities.

Sources: Stormvermin (`lpw-gluma-82593`), Clanrat (`lpw-gluma-80783`).

Warp Lightning Cannon replaces the animal body with a low four-wheel machine, but repeats dirty metal, brown timber and green accents. The bright green stone or energy form at the muzzle gives the device a focal colour that the ordinary body palette does not supply.

Sources: Warp Lightning Cannon (`lpw-gluma-145290`).
