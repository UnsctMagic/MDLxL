# Bretonnia: three commoners, not a knight-based faction sample

Scope: 3 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/bretonnia-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Bretonnian Archer, Pikeman and Swordsman share blue upper clothing, brown wrapped boots, a large square belt buckle and a small gold chest emblem. The archer has a hood, the pikeman a simple helmet, and the swordsman a tall blue shield; headgear and held equipment distinguish the three roles.

Sources: Bretonnian Archer (`lpw-herrdave-124832`), Bretonnian Pikeman (`lpw-herrdave-124833`), Bretonnian Swordsman (`lpw-herrdave-124831`).

The visible body proportions remain short-legged and broad-booted across the three published pictures. The pike extends far above the head, while the bow and shield create lateral rather than vertical mass. Their whole-model totals are 783, 825 and 896 triangles respectively. These are measured commoner examples, not a proposed generic humanoid budget.

Sources: Bretonnian Archer (`lpw-herrdave-124832`), Bretonnian Pikeman (`lpw-herrdave-124833`), Bretonnian Swordsman (`lpw-herrdave-124831`).
