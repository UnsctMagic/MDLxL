# Chaos: the marks change the visual grammar of a shared base

Scope: 20 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/warriors-of-chaos-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Knight of Khorne · Axe and Knight of Khorne · Champion put the strongest red on the upright head crest, chest and weapon. Gold outlines separate these red fields from the dark hanging mail. Their outward shoulder spikes and near-rectangular crown forks make the upper silhouette read as a wide block with two vertical prongs. Both credit Vile Knight as their model base.

Sources: Knight of Khorne · Axe (`lpw-khorne-axe`), Knight of Khorne · Champion (`lpw-khorne-flail`).

Knight of Nurgle, Heavy and Champion break that clean red-and-gold hierarchy with low-saturation olive plates, fleshy or mouth-like forms, irregular horns and much wider soft belly shapes. The credited sources explicitly include Ghoul/Abomination faces and a stone-deer statue for horns. A source object can therefore contribute its shape independently of the material painted on it.

Sources: Knight of Nurgle (`lpw-nurgle-standard`), Knight of Nurgle · Heavy (`lpw-nurgle-heavy`), Knight of Nurgle · Champion (`lpw-nurgle-champion`).

Knight of Slaanesh · Axe and Lance retain the shared armoured base but the large purple cloth area, bright gold bands, tall delicate horn sweep and face-bearing shield make a different mark readable. The axe and lance variants share their major silhouette landmarks; the long lance adds reach without requiring a wholesale body redesign. Their total meshes are 2,504 and 2,366 triangles respectively.

Sources: Knight of Slaanesh · Axe (`lpw-slaanesh-axe`), Knight of Slaanesh · Lance (`lpw-slaanesh-lance`).

Knight of Tzeentch · Sword/Lance and Warrior of Tzeentch use dark blue, cyan light, purple cloth and eyes as separate focal points. The visible eye animation is sourced from the Battle.net working animation texture series. The two knights each reference 30 eye-frame images; the warrior references 29. Counting those frames as unrelated painted materials exaggerates the number of visual ingredients.

Sources: Knight of Tzeentch · Sword (`lpw-tzeentch-sword`), Knight of Tzeentch · Lance (`lpw-tzeentch-lance`), Warrior of Tzeentch (`lpw-warrior-of-tzeentch`).

Knight Undivided · Sword/Lance and Aspiring Champion place black or charcoal plate beneath gold rims, horns and repeated star motifs. The palette creates a shared visual relationship without needing the red Khorne fields, green Nurgle flesh or cyan Tzeentch eyes. The credits separately name helmets, shoulder stars, horns and a fur cloak.

Sources: Knight Undivided · Sword (`lpw-undivided-sword`), Knight Undivided · Lance (`lpw-undivided-lance`), Aspiring Champion (`lpw-aspiring`).

Warrior of Khorne · Cleaver is 1,009 triangles while Berserker is 1,930 and Bruiser is 1,720. The cleaver still reads as the same heavily armoured family in the published pictures. The large continuous red blade carries much of its identity; total triangle count is not a direct measure of how ornate a character reads.

Sources: Warrior of Khorne · Cleaver (`lpw-warrior-of-khorne-cleaver`), Warrior of Khorne · Berserker (`lpw-warrior-of-khorne-berserker`), Warrior of Khorne · Bruiser (`lpw-warrior-of-khorne`).

## What this sample does not establish

These are observations of the 20 catalogue Chaos models. They do not establish a required Chaos triangle budget. The knight credits include mounted-base and horse sources, so each actual surface must be identified before interpreting an armour fragment as human anatomy.
