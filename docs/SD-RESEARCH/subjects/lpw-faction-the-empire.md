# Empire: equipment and heraldry carry role recognition

Scope: 28 downloaded catalogue models. Visual evidence: `D:/Codex/sd-research/pictures/lowpolyworks/the-empire-*.jpg`; all four study-rendered angles and UV overlays are in `captures/models/<id>/`. These observations describe the examples, not mandatory style rules.

Empire Captain, Halberdier and Swordsman combine a bright red cloth area with pale breastplate, a dark boot mass and a feather or plume high on the head. Shields carry broad readable cross divisions. The polearm and sword separate the role while the torso palette keeps the figures together.

Sources: Empire Captain (`lpw-herrdave-86791`), Empire Halberdier (`lpw-herrdave-88507`), Empire Swordsman (`lpw-herrdave-86747`).

Estalian Swordsman replaces the rectangular cross shield with a round concentric target, and Estalian Infantry/Spearman use a brimmed helmet rather than the same feathered profile. The distinctive equipment remains legible even though red, silver and brown remain shared colours.

Sources: Estalian Swordsman (`lpw-herrdave-88509`), Estalian Infantry (`lpw-herrdave-88510`), Estalian Spearman (`lpw-herrdave-88508`).

Knight of the Blazing Sun uses the head-sized gold sun crest as its strongest identifying motif in both mounted and dismounted versions. The dismounted model is 775 triangles; the mounted model is 1,713. The motif survives the much larger lower silhouette and is not dependent on adding tiny armour detail.

Sources: Knight of the Blazing Sun (Mounted) (`lpw-herrdave-81065`), Knight of the Blazing Sun (Dismounted) (`lpw-herrdave-76825`).

Gluma’s four Empire Handgunners give the weapon a deliberately large visual footprint. The arquebus, flintlock, scoped and multi-barrel variants retain a compact human body, while barrel length, muzzle grouping and the sight create the visible differences. The catalogue totals span 526–598 triangles for these four models.

Sources: Empire Handgunners (Arquebus) (`lpw-gluma-164659`), Empire Handgunners (Flintlock) (`lpw-gluma-164660`), Empire Handgunners (Scoped) (`lpw-gluma-164663`), Empire Handgunners (`lpw-gluma-164662`).

Human Cannon Bronze/Black and Human Mortar Bronze/Black share large wooden wheels, red hanging straps and a strongly outlined muzzle. The bronze/black variants have equal triangle counts within each pair (500 cannon, 482 mortar). The mortar changes the barrel angle and opening into a squat bowl-like shape, making function visible in silhouette.

Sources: Human Cannon Bronze (`lpw-gluma-146044`), Human Cannon Black (`lpw-gluma-146046`), Human Mortar Bronze (`lpw-gluma-146045`), Human Mortar Black (`lpw-gluma-146047`).

## What this sample does not establish

The Empire collection includes 28 models, but no Empire buildings. The observations cover troops, heroes and artillery represented here; colour in the published pictures includes the chosen team colour.
